# 0.2.4.114041

The Minecraft 1.14.4 port keeps the familiar pieces and placement, with
native waterlogging and BOP's own slabs, stairs and walls wherever they fit.

Cut retired rock slabs from two full blocks placed diagonally, and cut their
stairs from slabs. These cuts keep separate from Mineralogy's native recipes.

Older BOP pieces keep their saved orientation, counts and custom data. Materials
that BOP removed use similar current woods or coloured terracotta. Flower
Baskets keep their slots and contents, with old plant names converted before
loading. See [world upgrades](WORLD-UPGRADES.md) for the replacements and limits.

Supported old BOP terrain, plants, stored items and biome boundaries are
recovered too. Removed biomes use similar current biomes; snowy tundra keeps a
snowy replacement. Unrelated mods still need their own upgrade support.

Back up your world and test a copy before upgrading.
