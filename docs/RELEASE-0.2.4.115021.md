# 0.2.4.115021

This local candidate brings the BOP building pieces and Flower Basket to
Minecraft 1.15.2 and Forge 31.2.57. It requires Building Pieces 0.3.8.115021 and
BOP 10.0.0.366.

Ethereal wood uses birch replacements, with white cherry leaves and saplings.
Dried sand becomes dried salt and barley becomes tall wheat. Removed Nether
and End biomes use replacements in their original dimensions. Saved pieces,
inventories and Flower Basket contents retain their orientations, counts and
custom data.

Back up the world and test a separate copy before upgrading. See the world
[upgrade guide](WORLD-UPGRADES.md) for supported replacements and limits.
Manual testing is pending.
