# Trying the beta

Install the Minecraft 1.21.1 main jars for Building Pieces 0.3.9.121012,
this add-on, BOP 21.1.0.14, TerraBlender 4.1.0.8 and GlitchCore 2.1.0.2
on the client and server. Use a new world or a backup copy.

- Check wood and stone piece names in English and your usual game language.
  The names should describe the material and shape without repeating “block of”.
- Check stone walls in straight runs, corners
  and junctions. The panels should be lower than the posts. Add and remove a
  block or torch above the middle of a run, then check selection and collision.
- Craft the available wood and stone pieces using the core's ordinary patterns.
  Check quantities, native slab recipes and combining corners into steps, steps
  into slabs and slabs into full blocks. Check the recipe book too.
- Place and join slabs, vertical slabs, steps and corners in every direction.
- Check textures, native stairs, nonwood walls, breaking and item names.
- Use regular BOP slab items for vertical placement. Breaking or pick-block
  should return the regular slab, with no vertical slab creative entry.
- Place blocks above and beside pieces; exposed internal faces must remain visible.
- Check that hellbark does not burn, while other wooden pieces behave normally.
- On a 1.12 world copy, inspect retired woods, gems and thatching. They should
  use the replacements listed in the world-upgrade guide, keeping their shapes.
  Check old BOP terrain, plants and biome boundaries as well as building pieces.
- On separate world copies, compare replacement off and on, then save and reload.
- Inspect converted BOP builds and container contents, including counts and custom names.
- Check old cave formations, jelled poison pools and tree moss changed to willow
  vines. Save and reload, then check their positions and vine attachment faces.
- Craft a Flower Basket and open it from each hand. Try manual storage and plant
  pickup, including a nearly full basket. Rename it, store it in a chest and
  reload. Check that the open basket cannot be moved, swapped or thrown.
- On an old-world copy, check beetroot seeds from turnip seeds, two brown dyes
  per pinecone, and eight oxeye daisies per dull flower band. Include custom names and a stack that needs
  a spare inventory slot, then save and reload.

No terrain is generated. Wooden walls and unrelated biome materials are not included.
