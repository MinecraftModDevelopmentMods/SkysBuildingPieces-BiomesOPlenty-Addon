# 0.2.4.116051

This local candidate brings the BOP building pieces and Flower Basket to
Minecraft 1.16.5 and Forge 36.2.34. It requires Building Pieces 0.3.8.116051
and BOP 13.1.0.488.

The piece catalogue and saved identities stay the same. Plants and biomes
removed or renamed by BOP use the replacements listed in the
[upgrade guide](WORLD-UPGRADES.md). Basket contents keep their counts and
custom data.

Back up your world and test a separate copy before upgrading. Manual testing
is pending.
